The rules

A first date with a film.

  1. 01

    Read the story

    No title, no poster, no trailer. Just the plot, the mood, and the mess. Judge it like you'd judge a person on a first date.

  2. 02

    Guess if you can

    Some will feel familiar. Some will wreck you at 2 a.m. Some are here purely to make you say, “wait, that one?”

  3. 03

    Press reveal

    Get the title, year, IMDb rating, runtime, cast, and the one-line reason it will live in your head rent-free.
